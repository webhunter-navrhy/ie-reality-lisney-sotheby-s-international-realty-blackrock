A rare opportunity in the heart of Old Foxrock. Set on a mature site extending to approximately one third of an acre, this exceptional property presents a unique opportunity to create a substantial new family home on one of Foxrock's most prestigious residential avenues.

Full planning permission has been secured for a detached five-bedroom residence extending to approximately 250 sq.m. (2,700 sq. ft.) enabling the purchaser to build a contemporary A-rated family home within an established and highly sought-after setting.

The site enjoys a picturesque natural stream forming the rear boundary, creating a tranquil and private garden environment rarely found in such a prime suburban location. Mature planting and established surroundings further enhance the sense of seclusion and charm.

Old Foxrock is renowned for its tree-lined avenues, mature hedgerows, village atmosphere and strong sense of community. Residents enjoy easy access to Foxrock Village, the Luas, the M50 and Dublin City Centre, together with an exceptional selection of South Dublin's leading schools including Loreto Foxrock, Nord Anglia International School, Blackrock College, St Andrew's College and Mount Anville.

Foxrock's enduring appeal, beautifully maintained streetscapes and long-established reputation as one of Dublin's premier residential locations continue to attract discerning purchasers seeking an exceptional family environment.

Opportunities of this nature are exceptionally rare. With very few comparable residential sites remaining on Gordon Avenue, this property offers a unique chance to create a bespoke family home in one of South Dublin's most desirable and established addresses.
